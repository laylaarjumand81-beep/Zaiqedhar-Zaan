A Karachi kitchen with a warm table

Zaiqedar Zaan brings the taste of Karachi's busiest food streets into one welcoming dining room. Our cooks start early, grind spices in-house and cook each order fresh, from slow-simmered Nihari to crisp Zinger burgers.

Families, friends and late-night crowds all find something to love here. Sit down, share a few plates and take your time.

  • Cooked fresh to orderNothing waits under a lamp. Your food comes hot from the kitchen.
  • Spices ground in-houseOur karahi and Nihari get their flavour from whole spices.
  • Room for everyoneLarge tables for families and a quiet corner for two.
